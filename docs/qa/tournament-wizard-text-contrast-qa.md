# Tournament Wizard Text Contrast QA

## Desktop review

On the live Quickstart setup form, all primary labels, optional markers, helper content, placeholders, and tournament-setting controls remain readable against the dark green panel. The refined supporting hierarchy keeps the white title and form labels dominant while increasing low-emphasis text enough to scan without strain. The text change does not alter input sizes, field order, selection states, or the Wizard’s direct format-to-setup transition.
